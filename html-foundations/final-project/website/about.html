Our Story

Founded in 2026, Batol's Cafe began as a simple dream to bring authentic artisanal coffee to our community. We believe that every single cup tells a story, from the high-altitude farms where our beans are harvested to the careful roast that unlocks their rich, complex flavors.

Our founder spent years studying traditional brewing methods, ensuring that every batch meets our uncompromising standards of quality and sustainability. We are much more than just a place to grab a drink; we strive to be a welcoming gathering place for friends, creators, and neighbors alike.

To learn more about global coffee standards, agricultural practices, and roasting profiles, you can visit the National Coffee Association's About Coffee guide.

How We Make a Cappuccino

  1. Extract a fresh, rich double shot of espresso into a warmed ceramic cup.
  2. Texturize fresh whole milk using a steam wand until the microfoam becomes glossy and velvety.
  3. Pour the micro-foamed milk smoothly into the center of the espresso, balancing crema and milk.
  4. Finish with a light, delicate layer of airy foam centered perfectly on top.

Coffee Glossary

Espresso
A concentrated coffee beverage brewed by forcing hot water under high pressure through finely ground coffee beans.
Cappuccino
An espresso-based coffee drink traditionally prepared with equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and airy milk foam.
Flat White
An espresso beverage topped with steamed milk and a very thin layer of microfoam, offering a velvety texture and strong coffee flavor.
